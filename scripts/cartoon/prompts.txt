Prompts for the voyage intro's creature paintings. Each is style.txt plus one
block below, run with gen.sh from ~/Projects/portfolio-projects/_cartoon
(it asks Codex's image model for one image and saves it to raw/<name>.png).
Then run scripts/bake-cartoon.py from the repo root.

Fish and shark add: "IMPORTANT: this is a TOP-DOWN game sprite. The creature
must be perfectly MIRROR-SYMMETRIC about the horizontal midline ... NOTHING
seen from the side: no fin pokes out to only one side."

orca2   Background #00FF00, fills ~85% of the width. An adult killer whale
        (orca) character seen from directly above: glossy blue-black back,
        white eye patches on both sides behind the eyes, pale grey saddle
        patch behind the dorsal fin, dorsal fin seen from above as a narrow
        dark blade on the midline, broad rounded pectoral flippers out to the
        sides a third of the way back, flukes spread sideways like a
        swept-back crescent with a notch. Rounded blunt head at the right.

jelly   Background #00FF00, painted opaque. A moon jellyfish from directly
        above as SEPARATE PARTS: (A) the bell, a round glowing pink-lilac
        dome with a four-petal clover pattern and a scalloped rim; (B) four
        frilly ribbon-like oral arms lying horizontally, root on the left.

fish    Background #FF00FF. Two schooling fish from directly above, pointing
        right, one above the other: (A) golden-yellow with an orange spine
        stripe and blue-edged fins; (B) silver-blue with a teal back. Forked
        tails spread flat sideways.

shark   Background #FF00FF, fills ~85% of the width. A friendly blue shark
        from directly above: slate-blue back, lighter flanks, pointed snout at
        the right with an eye each side, dorsal fin as a thin blade on the
        midline, long swept-back pectoral fins, small pelvic fins, tail fin
        edge-on at the left.
