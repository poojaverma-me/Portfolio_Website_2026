15 cents a million: MiniMax vs DeepSeek, Argon’s two ARC scores and Anthropic’s 5% pledge

AI work keeps getting cheaper, but the numbers used to judge it depend on who picked the rivals, which harness ran the test, and whether a pledge has been voted on yet.

The short version

  • MiniMax M3.1 Flash launched at a flat $0.15 / $0.60 per million tokens, the same as DeepSeek V4.1-Flash-1017’s off-peak price but with no peak surcharge. vendor
  • DeepSeek still leads on coding, even on MiniMax’s own DeepSWE chart, and Artificial Analysis ranks it 45 to MiniMax’s 42. vendor independent
  • Gemini 4 Argon scored 97.5% and 49.8% on ARC-AGI-3: the same model with two different harnesses. independent
  • Four Argon agents beat Minecraft in 27 hours 14 minutes, with no human help, for about $520 at introductory prices. vendor
  • Anthropic pledged 5% of its shares to a new Public Benefit Fund, subject to a stockholder vote. vendor

01Space Bunny was MiniMax

In late September an unnamed, free model called Space Bunny appeared on OpenRouter. OpenRouter says it was the platform’s #1 model for 10 straight days and processed 56.7 trillion tokens, the most of any stealth model it has hosted. It came off OpenRouter on 5 Oct. independent On 19 Oct MiniMax revealed it as an early version of M3.1 Flash, saying it processed “more than 200 trillion tokens” while anonymous. vendor

The final model is free on OpenRouter for 72 hours. independent MiniMax says weights “will be released later”, with no licence named yet, and a Pro model is next. vendor

07Four Argon agents beat Minecraft

In a Google DeepMind paper (arXiv 2610.22319), Maxence Faldor, Satinder Singh and Roberta Raileanu placed four Gemini 4 Argon agents in a random Minecraft survival world on Easy, with one goal: defeat the Ender Dragon. They had no assigned roles and no game-specific tools; they acted by writing code through the standard Mineflayer library and coordinated over in-game chat and shared notes. vendor

  • Self-organised: within ten minutes the agents had split tools, food, diamonds and the portal between them. vendor
  • Geometry: one agent triangulated the stronghold from two Eye of Ender throws; its estimate landed 43 m from the target, 1.3 km away. vendor
  • Totals: 27 h 14 min, 2,783 programs, 229 chat messages and 605 deaths; 22% of programs ended in an error. The End fight took nearly 14 hours. vendor
  • No cheating found: a fifth Observer agent and a manual audit found no cheating or specification gaming. vendor

The cost: the paper estimates about $520 for the winning run at Argon’s introductory price ($1,040 at standard) and about $810 for the full session ($1,620). vendor That is roughly $19 an hour for the winning run, a figure this newsletter derived; the paper doesn’t state one. Some coverage attached the $810 full-session figure to the winning run. press

08Anthropic pledges 5% of itself

  • The pledge: subject to stockholder approval, 5% of Anthropic’s shares go to the Anthropic Public Benefit Fund, with 2% more at the board’s discretion for every 50% rise in the company’s value above a baseline. The baseline isn’t disclosed. vendor
  • The structure: a wholly owned LLC, not a 501(c)(3), with no lobbying or political giving. Governance and recipients come “in the coming months”. vendor
  • The total: with the co-founders’ 80% pledge and an employee donation match used by about half of staff, roughly 20% of shares are now dedicated to the public good. vendor
  • On paper: at the $965B post-money valuation of May’s Series H, 5% is about $48B. Anthropic gives no dollar figure; the calculation is this newsletter’s. vendor

Two days earlier, Anthropic’s own employment report said the junior share of its technical hires fell from 27% to 9%, and the junior share of technical employees from 21% to 14% since early 2025, while the company tripled in size. By August, agents were logging six times the hours of the employees directing them. vendor The fund announcement doesn’t reference the report. Elon Musk responded that the shares should go directly to the public, Cointelegraph reports. press

09Handle with care

  • Every MiniMax benchmark is vendor-run; independent coverage so far is Artificial Analysis and Code Arena.
  • An “Oct 23” date for MiniMax’s weights appears only in press reports; MiniMax says “later”.
  • MiniMax’s 428B / 23B-active size comes from a product guide, not a model card.
  • Argon’s rank on ARC’s standard-harness leaderboard depends on whether effort levels are listed separately, so it isn’t given here.
  • The Minecraft costs are the authors’ estimates from token counts; the run was on Easy, through code rather than vision.
  • The $48B figure is derived, and the pledge depends on a stockholder vote that hasn’t happened.
  • Anthropic’s report gives no dates, definitions or raw counts for the 27% → 9% hiring figure.
